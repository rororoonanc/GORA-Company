Our Story

About GORA

The name GORA is derived from TANGORA, the company’s first product. The name also comes from the Filipino expression “Gora,” which is a playful way of saying “Go.” It represents the company’s goal of encouraging people to move forward, explore new ideas, and go further. The name reflects the company’s willingness to create products that are innovative, useful, and meaningful to consumers.

Our Product

Our Product: TANGORA

TANGORA will be available in 50 mL and 100 mL sizes to provide consumers with options based on their needs and budget. The product is intended for indoor spaces such as homes, apartments, condominiums, offices, and other establishments. Its effectiveness may vary depending on factors such as room size, number of diffuser sticks used, and the level of mosquito presence.
